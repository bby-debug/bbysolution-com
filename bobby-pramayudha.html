01 — Profile

About

Bobby has spent over three decades in the engineering design and construction of onshore and offshore structures for oil & gas, petrochemical, and industrial facilities — from conceptual design through detailed analysis, reassessment, and construction supervision, across steel, concrete, and geotechnical work.

Beyond core structural work, Bobby has repeatedly stepped into director, project engineer, and construction management roles — building execution plans, controlling budgets, sitting on bidding committees, and coordinating multi-discipline teams across joint ventures and multi-office locations for clients including Kellogg Brown & Root, McDermott, and Kvaerner.
